REFUND ELIGIBILITY
- Payments are generally non-refundable. Applicable legal and contractual rights still apply.
- OpenAI may also approve refunds for a verified OpenAI billing error, validated unauthorized charges, or a documented OpenAI service or access failure.
- For an unused individual subscription, a discretionary refund may be available if the customer contacts OpenAI within 7 days of the charge.
- The request must concern the current billing period and meet eligibility requirements: limits on repeat refunds, no substantial use, no active payment dispute, no confirmed abuse.
- The request should include the reason and confirm that refunded subscription access will end.
- There is no automatic prorated discretionary refund after 7 days.
- An accidental purchase, forgetting to cancel, dissatisfaction, or a plan change does not by itself qualify, unless an applicable exception applies.
- If a refund is granted, access covered by the refunded charge normally ends.
- Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals. It does not establish refund eligibility.

PAYMENT DISPUTES
- OpenAI cannot issue a separate refund while a payment dispute is active, or after funds were already returned through the dispute process.

HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND, BY PLACE OF PURCHASE
- Purchased on the web (chatgpt.com): log in to ChatGPT, then open the Help Center chat widget (bottom right of help.openai.com) while logged in to the same account that was charged. The system shows a summary of refund eligibility before connecting to an agent. If eligible, OpenAI processes the refund within 5-7 business days. The time for funds to appear also depends on the bank. A receipt is emailed to the account email.
- Purchased on Google Play: same chat route as the web. If eligible, OpenAI processes the refund within 10 business days.
- Purchased on the Apple App Store: cancellations and refunds must be requested directly from Apple.

REGIONAL NOTES
- EU, UK and Turkey: a prorated refund is available if the subscription is cancelled within 14 days of purchase.
- Republic of Korea: a full refund is available within 7 days of purchase if the service was not used; otherwise a pro-rated refund applies.

NOT COVERED IN THESE NOTES
- ChatGPT Business seat and workspace refunds (see the live page).
